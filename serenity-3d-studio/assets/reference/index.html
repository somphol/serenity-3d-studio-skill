    How to practise

    1. Choose a visualization: clouds for expansive awareness, a stream for non-attachment, or the orb for breath-led grounding.
    2. Pick one of the twenty practices and a session length. Cues scale evenly into the time you chose.
    3. When a thought pulls at you, type it and choose Thought, Emotion or Body. It is reframed as “I notice…” and released into the scene.
    4. Follow the narration, or mute it and read the cue card. Breathe with the small orb if you chose the sanctuary.
    5. End the session to record your streak. Export the JSON log any time from Settings.

    This is a self-reflection tool, not medical care. If thoughts feel overwhelming, step away and reach out to someone you trust or a qualified professional.
